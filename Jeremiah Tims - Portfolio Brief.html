Full Payment-to-Provision UI/UX

23 steps to 1 clickV1 live in 5 days · Zero downtime since launch · PCI/PII compliance

A self-service path for low-priced product lines. Discovery with the VP of RevOps, the CRO and the product SME separated the steps the business needed from the ones the manual process had accreted; the target flow ran across a checkout front end, an application database, Stripe for payments, n8n for the handoffs and Salesforce as the system of record for sales and finance. How the Work Is Done

Every build above was produced under the same discipline: the ask restated and counted, load-bearing claims labeled by how they are known, verification by an independent path, an adversarial pass before shipping and a written record of what was and was not executed. The code is written with an AI agent harness and I answer for every line in review. The operating manual in the public repositories is the runbook; the work is the proof.
